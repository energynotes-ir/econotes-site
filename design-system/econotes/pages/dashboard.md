# Dashboard Page Rules

## Purpose and audience

Serve macroeconomic analysts, portfolio managers and finance leaders who need a traceable view of Iran's economy and broad market conditions. Keep the product focused on macro relationships rather than individual-stock recommendations.

## Visual hierarchy

1. Put the selected analytical area and its time scope first.
2. Show source, observation date, unit and frequency near each series value.
3. Group comparable risk and relationship charts in aligned grids with shared date controls.
4. Keep summary cards compact so they support the charts instead of competing with them.

## Language and evidence

- Use Persian labels and plain, specific descriptions. Retain standard technical acronyms such as OLS, VaR and YTM where useful, and define them nearby.
- Distinguish observation date from retrieval time and distinguish reported values from derived estimates.
- Describe the current scenario calculation as historical, simultaneous and descriptive. Do not call it a forecast or imply causality.
- State sample size, frequency, coverage limits and known data caveats where they affect interpretation.
- Use exact units and avoid unsupported claims about prediction accuracy or investment performance.

## Interaction and accessibility

- Use the shared light palette and the locally hosted Vazirmatn font from the master design system.
- Use blue for primary series, amber for events and cautions, teal for positive changes, and red for negative changes. Never encode meaning with color alone.
- Keep chart tooltips legible and include both chart date and original observation date when they differ.
- Preserve keyboard focus, visible controls, reduced-motion support, and mobile layouts without page-level horizontal overflow.
- Motion should confirm state changes; avoid decorative animation in analytical views.
